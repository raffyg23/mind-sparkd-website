MindSparkd

A quote a day,
and somewhere to keep it.

MindSparkd gives you one line at a time to read, sit with, and write back to. Like the ones that land, reflect on the ones that stay, and set them to come back when you need them.

What it does

  • A feed you swipe — one quote at a time, over a background you choose.
  • Thirty genres — from motivation and gratitude to gaming, gym, pets and everything between.
  • A journal — everything you liked or wrote about, kept together.
  • Reminders — bring a saved quote back once, daily, or on a schedule you set.

Built to leave you alone

There are no analytics, no tracking and no advertising in MindSparkd. Your reminders are scheduled by your own phone, not sent from a server. What you write stays yours, and you can delete all of it from inside the app at any time.
