  • Like the ones that land

    One quote at a time, full screen, over a background you pick. Swipe up for the next.

  • Reflect on a ruled page

    Tap the pen and write what the line meant to you. It stays with the quote in your journal, sorted by month.

  • Bring it back when you need it

    Set a saved quote to return once, daily, or on your own schedule, right on your lock screen.

  • Share it as an image

    Send a quote, with your background and your reflection, as a picture made for stories.

  • Keep your streak

    A like or a reflection a day keeps it going. Small habit, real momentum.
